Paint Patch is being built as a private iPhone notebook for your home paint tests and touch-ups. Plan a room, photograph real wall samples under labeled lighting, keep the paint codes and sheen exactly as printed on your cans, and compare your own observations without trusting a phone photo as an exact color match. A local touch-up history and portable inventory/backup are planned so your records remain yours. No account or cloud service is planned. This is draft listing copy for a planned app; review every feature against the implemented release before publication.
